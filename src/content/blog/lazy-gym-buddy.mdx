The hardest part of exercise isn't the workout. It's getting up off the sofa to start one.

Lazy Gym Buddy is built around that moment. Its whole cast is one panda, Bao, who loves working out and is far too lazy to do it alone. He'll go if you go.

## Getting up together

Bao starts on the sofa. Tap to get him up, or tickle his belly until he gives in. Then work out, any way you like, and tap when you're done. Bao celebrates with you, then naps like he earned it.

## No guilt

There are no streaks to lose and no reminders telling you you've failed. When you skip a day, Bao skips it too. Your week shows the days you moved, and nothing else.

## Works with Apple Health

Bao reads your workouts and steps from Apple Health, so a run tracked on an Apple Watch or in another app counts too. The app only reads from Health and never writes to it.

## Private

There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Your health data stays on your iPhone.

## When you can meet Bao

Lazy Gym Buddy is coming to the App Store for iPhone.
