Most chess apps feel loud. There are timers, ratings, streaks and a badge for every notification. That works if you already play. When you're learning, it's a lot.

Calm Chess is built for the second case. It doesn't rush you, and it helps you see what's happening on the board.

## A coach after every move

After each move, the coach tells you whether it was great, good or a slip, and why, in one short sentence. If you're stuck, ask for a hint. If you make a mistake, undo it and try again. You can also step back through a finished game to see where it turned.

## Opponents that grow with you

You start against the Rookie, who you can beat in your first game. Win three games and the Master unlocks, then the Grandmaster. If a friend is next to you, pass the phone and play each other.

## Seeing the board

Lines of light show where a piece can go. Hold a piece to see what guards it and what attacks it. While the engine thinks, its candidate moves appear on the board, so you can watch it weigh its options.

## Private and offline

There's no account and there are no ads. The engine runs on your phone, so Calm Chess works on a plane, and your game is saved on the device where you left it.

## Three languages

Calm Chess speaks English, Hindi and Spanish. Hindi came early because, by Google Trends, interest in chess is higher in India than in the United States.
